## 📌 Overview

**CensusSaathi AI** is a GenAI-powered web application created for the **Census 2027 & Digital Enumeration** challenge.

The goal is simple:

> Make Census 2027 information easier for every citizen to understand and access.

The platform brings important census guidance, state-wise schedules, self-enumeration assistance, AI-powered question answering, misinformation awareness, multilingual support, privacy education, and data visualization into one easy-to-use interface.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Census participation can be confusing for citizens because information may be distributed across different sources and presented in complex formats.

Citizens may need help understanding:

- What the census is
- How the different census phases work
- When they need to participate
- How self-enumeration works
- What information may be requested
- How to stay safe from scams and misinformation
- How census data can be understood
- Where to find trustworthy information
- How to access information in their preferred Indian language

CensusSaathi AI addresses these challenges through a single citizen-focused digital experience.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

CensusSaathi AI acts as a **digital companion for Census 2027**.

Instead of forcing users to search through complicated information, the platform provides:

### 🧠 Understand
Simple explanations of Census 2027 and its phases.

### 📅 Check
State-wise schedule and census-related information.

### 📝 Complete
A guided self-enumeration experience and demonstration flow.

### 🤖 Ask
An AI assistant that answers census-related questions.

### 🔎 Verify
A claim checker that helps users identify potentially misleading census messages.

### 📊 Explore
Interactive visualizations that make census data easier to understand.

### 🌐 Access
Multilingual support for Indian languages.

### 🔐 Stay Safe
Privacy and digital-safety guidance for citizens.
